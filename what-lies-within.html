What Lies Within

What Lies Within invites viewers to search beneath appearances. Its title frames close looking as an act of discovery: what appears to be a surface can also suggest an opening into another layer of the image.

Underwater-inspired atmosphere and layered forms connect the artwork to Kimberly Nichole Reising’s exploration of depth, concealment and delayed recognition. The composition encourages attention to what is visible and what remains unresolved.

Across KNR Studio, organic imagery and visual architecture often exchange roles. What Lies Within belongs to that conversation between an immediately legible subject and the structures that emerge with time.
