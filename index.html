About the Artist

Kimberly Nichole Reising is a Philadelphia-based visual artist and fine art model working across graphite, colored pencil, mixed media, and digital rendering.

A graduate of the University of the Arts Philadelphia, her work moves through surrealism, dark fantasy, bio-organic form, and a self-described "female noir" sensibility — figures caught mid-transformation, rendered in thread, light, and unresolved edges.

Reising is the grand prize winner of Philadelphia's America 250 design contest. Her work has shown at Philadelphia City Hall and been submitted to national and regional juried exhibitions.
