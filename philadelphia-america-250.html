Philadelphia America 250

Philadelphia America 250 marks a milestone through Kimberly Nichole Reising’s visual approach to commemorative artwork. The piece brings a Philadelphia perspective to the themes of celebration, history and creative expression.

Reising’s America 250 design received first-place recognition in Philadelphia and was publicly displayed near City Hall. That public presentation connects the work to the city in which the artist develops her wider surrealist and mixed-media practice.
